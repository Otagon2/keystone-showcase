For the review · Day 5 — what to look at first

The next step is a walkthrough with Zaki — here's the three-minute path through today's work, and the decisions worth ratifying.

Day 5The self-audit → seven gaps closedReady to review

The three-minute path: open Connect (first-run) and click Simulate first trace (the new demo opener) → Playground → Compare and add the v11 variant (A/B/n with a Promote-winner button) → Monitor → Online evals (the loop staying on in production) → agree with the judge on trace gst-003, then open the evaluator and scroll to Alignment — your verdict is already in the judge-vs-human matrix.

Decisions to ratify
  • Connect as the on-ramp — one-line setup + "unlocks with your first trace" (the pattern our teardown rated best-in-class at Braintrust, done clearer).
  • Cost speaks tokens only — every fabricated dollar removed; task-vs-judge split everywhere (our own principle, now consistently applied).
  • Judge-the-judge — human verdicts persist and grade the evaluator (agreement %, too-lenient cell, linked disagreements). Neither competitor closes this loop.
  • The import mapper redesign — per-column dropdowns with sample values replace drag-to-bucket (from Amaan's live review; also fixes the dialog closing mid-drag).
Open for later
  • Full RBAC/audit/billing (Settings shows an honest roadmap panel today) · natural-language log filters (positioned as the copilot's job) · Operate / Learn planes.
